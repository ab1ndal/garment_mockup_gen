TITLE DETAIL ("title_detail"): a short phrase that completes this product's website title. The site builds the title as: {colour, if the product has exactly one} {fabric} {category} with {title_detail}. Its job is to tell this piece apart from other products of the same fabric and category.

- Say what makes this piece recognisable: its work, border, print, motif or weave, and where the work sits when that is what stands out. Use the parts this kind of garment really has (a pallu only on a saree or dupatta; a yoke, neckline, cuffs or jacket on a ready garment).
- At most 60 characters, usually 2 to 5 words. Title Case. Plain words only, no marketing words (elegant, stunning, adorable, beautiful, premium, exclusive).
- Do not start with "with"; the title adds it.
- Do not repeat the garment's main colour, its fabric or its category; the title adds those.
- If the catalogue lists more than one colour, one page covers all of them, so use no colour words. Gold, golden and silver are allowed only for metallic work that is on every design. If it lists one colour, an accent that differs from it is fine ("Black Border").
- Only state what the original photos or the shop's own text show. Earlier AI descriptions often contradict the photo because the same design comes in several colours; when a description and a photo disagree, follow the photo.
- Use the standard spelling of craft terms even when the shop's text spells them differently: Gharchola, Kantha, Gota Patti, Zari, Resham, Bandhej, Swarovski, Buti.
- Do not claim an origin (Banarasi, Patola as genuine) unless the owner note or the shop's own text, not an earlier AI description, states it. Say "Patola Pattern", not "Patola".
- Never mention stitched, semi-stitched, unstitched, sizes, price or occasion.
- If nothing distinctive can be seen, use an empty string; a reviewer will write it.
